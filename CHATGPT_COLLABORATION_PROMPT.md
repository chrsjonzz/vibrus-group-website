## 3. CRITICAL DEFICIENCIES OF THE CURRENT SITE (Must Be Fixed 10/10)

1. **Outdated Platform:** Built in 2017 on 1&1 IONOS SiteBuilder (`201.sb.mywebsite-editor.com`), leaving exposed editor session fragments and non-semantic tables.
2. **SEO Catastrophe:** The homepage meta description literally reads: *"Vibrus Group in Southfield offers services for business and finance"* — this is completely misleading, Southfield is an old address (HQ is Warren, MI), and it completely omits Healthcare and Industrial staffing.
3. **Outdated Clinical Terminology:** Mentions "ICD9/CPT Coders". ICD-9 was federally replaced by ICD-10 in 2015. We must modernize to ICD-10 / ICD-11 & CPT.
4. **Severe Candidate Friction:** The current "Apply" button abruptly redirects candidates off-site to a clunky third-party Jobsoid board (`vibrus.jobsoid.com`), causing severe drop-off for mobile clinicians and shift workers.
5. **Absence of B2B Client Ordering:** Healthcare facility staffing coordinators and warehouse managers currently only have a generic 3-field contact form with a captcha. There is zero ability to quickly order a shift, request an urgent rate card, or upload a requisition.
6. **No Real-Time Trust Architecture:** The site lacks visible credentialing badges, live dispatch indicators, interactive county coverage maps, or mobile-first design.
